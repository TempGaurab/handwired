Its rules, strongest first

    Each rule holds on a square or doesn’t, and a square’s score adds a weight for every rule that holds, each weight larger than all the ones below it together. The bar in each square is the score it got on the network’s last move, and an argmax tournament picks the highest.

    A rule as ReLUs

    // your code: O has two in a line, X none
oc === 2 && xc === 0

// turns into, with s = oc - 3 × xc
relu(s - 1) - relu(s - 2)

    Any X in the line pushes s below 0, so s is 2 only for two O and no X, and the two ReLUs make the step at 2. Forks count such lines through a square with one more step.

    The network

    weights · neurons · layers

    One evaluation takes your move, checks the board, scores all nine squares, runs the argmax and makes the network’s move. The later layers are the tournament: pairs of scores compared, then the winner’s path routed back to one square.
