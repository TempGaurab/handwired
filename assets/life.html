Game of Life

Conway’s rules as 1,536 hand-wired neurons. No training, and exact on every cell.

Try
  • Draw on the board
  • Press Play, or step one generation
  • Pick a pattern

Generation 0 · 0 alive · – per generation

One cell, one neuron

// your code
next = alive ? n === 2 || n === 3 : n === 3

// turns into, with s = 2 × n + alive
next = relu(s - 4) - relu(s - 5)
     - relu(s - 7) + relu(s - 8)

A live cell survives with 2 or 3 neighbors, so at s = 5 or 7. A dead cell is born with 3, at s = 6. So the whole rule is “s is from 5 to 7”, and for a whole number s that is the difference of two ReLU steps, which is exactly 1 or 0.

s is a 3 × 3 convolution: weight 2 on the 8 neighbors and 1 on the cell itself. The board wraps around at its edges, so a glider that leaves on the right comes back on the left.

Drawing

Your pen is in the network too. A press reads the cell under it with one table read and remembers the opposite value. A drag writes that value into every cell it crosses, through a select on each cell’s next state.
