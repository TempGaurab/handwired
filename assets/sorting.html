Sorting chart

A bar chart that keeps itself sorted while you drag. items.sort() without a loop: three layers deep.

  • Keep dragging as it slides away
  • Shuffle, or flip the order

Sort without a loop

// your code
items.sort((a, b) => a - b)

// turns into, for each pair i < j, once
before = [v[j] < v[i]]
rank[i] += before
rank[j] += 1 - before
x[i] = 36 × rank[i]

A bar’s place is how many bars come before it, so 16 bars need 120 comparisons, all side by side. Heights are whole pixels, which makes each comparison 2 ReLUs and the whole sort 3 layers. Equal bars keep their order, the way sort is stable.

The bar you hold follows you up and down, and its rank changes under your pointer, so it slides away sideways while you still hold it. A press grabs the bar by where it’s going, not where it’s drawn, and changes nothing by itself: the drag is relative to where you pressed.

Springs

Each bar’s x and height are springs, 32 in all, stepped inside the same evaluation by a 2 × 2 matrix that the page writes for the time since the last one. They land exactly on their slots, and then the page stops drawing.
